Certificates Expiry Management

For Customers / Vendors / Contacts / Employees / Users

This app will allow you to manage certificates and licenses of your customers/vendors/contacts as well as Employees / Users. Using this single you will be able to manage your certificate and license documents into Odoo. It also allows you to manage expiry dates and notifications for reminders for expiry soon and expired documents.


Main Features:

  • Allow you to manage licenses and expiry notifications for licenses. You can manage the licenses of your employees, customers/vendors/contacts, and users.
  • Allow you to manage certificates and expiry notifications for a license. You can manage the certificates of your employees, customers/vendors/contacts, and users.
  • Cron job will run daily to send notification to remind you to expire soon and expired status of certificates and licenses.
  • You can attach documents for the certificate and license form.
  • Your internal employee can view their own certificates and licenses in the backend.
  • Your customers/vendors/Users/Contacts can view their own certificates and licenses in frontend my account portal using portal login.
  • Workflow on certificates and licenses form as shown in the below screenshots.
  • Allow you to print PDFs of certificates and licenses as shown in the below format.
  • Certificates and Licenses Manager group created in a system that allows you to assign the certificates and licenses team to have full access.
  • Allow your manager to create and manage certificates and license types.
  • For more details please watch the video and check the below screenshots.
