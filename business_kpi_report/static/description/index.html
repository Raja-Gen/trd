Business KPI Daily Reports

Automated KPI snapshots (email / SMS), Monitor Sales, Website, POS, Accounting and more.

Key Features

Stop digging through menus and get your business vitals delivered straight to your inbox or phone.
Pick and choose exactly what you need from Sales, Accounting, POS, Web, and Payments with limitless KPI combinations to fit your style.
Stay updated in real-time on everything from revenue and new orders to visitor counts and pending invoices.
Track your progress your way with flexible reporting by the day, week, month, or even year.
Get company-wise clean summaries delivered instantly via Email, SMS, or both wherever you are.
Manage your whole empire with consolidated reports across all your companies from one simple setup.

How it Works?

1 Configure Settings (Disable / Enable)

Use the Settings page to enable the KPI reports and choose which companies and users receive them. Toggle the main "Enable Daily Business Performance Report" option to enable or disable automated delivery.

2 Select Delivery Options & KPIs

Once active, select your KPIs, delivery method (Email/SMS), and duration. Importantly, you can choose specific companies to receive reports company-wise or all companies.

3 KPI List View

Browse available KPIs in the list view. KPIs are organized by category (Sales, Website, POS, Accounting, Payments, Purchase) — pick the ones you want in email/sms.

4 Adjust Report Schedule

If you'd like, you can modify the time and frequency at which the KPI report is sent. Simply activate the developer mode and go to the Scheduled Actions section, find the action named "Daily Business KPI Report", and adjust its schedule as needed.

5 Email Preview

Monitor your business performance with an organized email summary displaying all your selected KPIs in one view.

6 SMS Preview

Monitor your business performance with an organized SMS summary displaying all your selected KPIs in one view.
